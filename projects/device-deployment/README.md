# Large-Scale Device Imaging and Deployment

## Context

This case study summarizes technical internship work completed at VSTECS KU Sdn. Bhd. The public version focuses on technical skills and measurable outcomes while excluding confidential customer, school, inventory, and company information.

## Scope

- More than 900 HP desktops
- More than 300 HP notebooks
- Approximately 190 ASUS notebooks
- Device deployments across schools in Putrajaya and Pahang

## Responsibilities

- Unboxed, assembled, and prepared devices for imaging.
- Configured BIOS settings and selected appropriate boot options.
- Performed operating-system imaging and post-image verification.
- Identified corrupted installations and re-imaged affected devices.
- Checked hardware condition and prepared verified devices for repacking and staging.
- Installed antivirus software and applied asset labels during field deployment.
- Maintained serial-number records, inventory checks, handover forms, and close-out files.

## Troubleshooting Examples

- Imaging failures or corrupted installations
- BIOS and boot-order problems
- Device-verification failures
- Hardware and peripheral issues
- Documentation or inventory mismatches

## Results

- Supported preparation and verification of more than 1,390 devices.
- Contributed to multi-location school deployments in Putrajaya and Pahang.
- Helped maintain consistent deployment records and audit-ready documentation.
- Worked effectively with technical teams during high-volume and outstation assignments.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Hardware imaging and BIOS configuration
- Technical troubleshooting
- Quality verification
- Asset management and documentation
- Teamwork, adaptability, and field deployment support
